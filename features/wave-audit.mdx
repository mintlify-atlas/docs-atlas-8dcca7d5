The AI Wave Audit is AI Surfer's signature diagnostic tool — a five-question business questionnaire that produces an **AI Opportunity Score™** out of 100 and maps your biggest growth lever to a specific AI Surfer agent. Unlike a generic quiz, every answer feeds a weighted scoring algorithm that evaluates your business across five capability categories: leads, operations, content, support, and visibility. When you finish, you receive a teaser Wave Map immediately and a complete 30-day Wave Plan delivered to your inbox.

<Note>
  The Wave Audit is a separate, deeper product from the free [Wave Check](/features/wave-check). Wave Check is a 3-question readiness quiz; the Wave Audit is a 5-question scored assessment that produces a full AI Wave Report. The full Wave Audit report is available for **$97** as part of the AEO Wave Audit package.
</Note>

## The Five Audit Questions

The audit walks you through five targeted questions. Each answer option is carefully weighted to reflect real AI opportunity signals in your business context.

## How Scoring Works

Your AI Opportunity Score™ is calculated out of 100. The algorithm starts with a base score of **28** and adds weighted bonus points for each answer. Larger teams, multi-location operations, and businesses with multiple high-friction areas accumulate higher scores because they have more surface area for AI to improve.

### Opportunity Levels

After calculating the raw score plus a category-normalisation bonus, answers are mapped to one of three opportunity levels:

| Score Range | Label |
|-------------|-------|
| 82 – 100 | High opportunity |
| 65 – 81 | Strong opportunity |
| Below 65 | Emerging opportunity |

## Category Scoring and Recommended Agents

In parallel with the overall score, the algorithm accumulates points across five **business capability categories**. Answers to questions 3, 4, and 5 contribute category points. If you answer "a little of everything" or "several areas" to two or more of those questions, the system flags a multi-area transformation scenario and recommends **Big Kahuna** instead of a single-category agent.

| Category | Label | Recommended Agent |
|----------|-------|------------------|
| `leads` | Lead & Sales Follow-Up | Sales Rider |
| `operations` | Workflow Automation | Automation Architect |
| `content` | Content & Marketing | Content Creator |
| `support` | Customer Care | Customer Care Cove |
| `visibility` | Opportunity Discovery | Wave Scout |

### The `WaveAuditResult` Shape

The scoring function returns a typed result object with the following fields:

```typescript src/features/wave-audit/scoring.ts
type WaveAuditResult = {
  score: number;          // 0–100, the AI Opportunity Score™
  topCategory: string;    // Human-readable category label
  opportunities: string[]; // 1–2 actionable opportunity sentences
  recommendedAgent: AuditAgent; // e.g. "Sales Rider" | "Big Kahuna"
  confidenceLabel: string; // "High opportunity" | "Strong opportunity" | "Emerging opportunity"
}
```

## User Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer Question 1 of 5">
    Select your business type. The answer is recorded and the questionnaire immediately advances to question 2 after a 180 ms transition. A progress bar tracks completion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer Questions 2 – 5">
    Work through team size, time drain, lost opportunity, and AI priority. You can step back at any time using the Back button to revise a previous answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Your Teaser Wave Map">
    Once all five answers are recorded, your AI Opportunity Score™ and top category are calculated client-side and displayed instantly — no server round trip required. You see your score out of 100, your opportunity level, two opportunity insights, and your recommended agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Email">
    To unlock the full 30-day Wave Plan, enter your email address. Clicking **Open My Full AI Wave Report** submits the email, answers, and scored result to Supabase via `saveWaveAuditLead`. A unique `submissionId` (UUID) is generated per submission to prevent duplicate records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Full Wave Report">
    After the lead is confirmed saved, the `FullWaveReport` component renders in-page. Your full report includes your 30-day Wave Plan, a practical first move, and details on the AI Surfer service that matches your biggest opportunity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email Capture and Lead Storage

The email step is intentionally placed *after* the questionnaire so users experience the score before being asked for contact details. The platform generates a UUID `submissionId` on form submit. If the Supabase write fails, the UI surfaces a retry prompt — the submission ID ensures the retry cannot create a duplicate record.

```typescript src/features/wave-audit/leadCapture.ts
await saveWaveAuditLead({
  email,
  answers,    // WaveAuditAnswers — all five responses
  result,     // WaveAuditResult — score, category, recommended agent
  source: "wave-audit",
  submissionId: crypto.randomUUID(),
});
```

<Tip>
  If your business spans multiple high-friction areas — answering "several areas" or "a little of everything" on two or more of questions 3–5 — the algorithm skips the single-category recommendation and suggests **Big Kahuna**, AI Surfer's full multi-area transformation package.
</Tip>
