Online reviews are one of the highest-leverage assets a local or service business can build — and most businesses lose them simply because they never ask at the right moment. The AI Review Booster Agent solves that problem by automating the entire review lifecycle: identifying customers most likely to leave a positive review, reaching out through the right channel at the right time, personalising every message, and responding professionally to reviews as they arrive. Built on the `otd-review-booster-agent` skill, it runs continuously so your review count grows even when your team is busy with everything else.

## Purpose

The Review Booster Agent exists to **help businesses increase positive customer reviews**. Its three goals are concrete and measurable: grow your Google review count, build customer trust, and improve your visibility in local search results.

## Full Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify Happy Customers">
    The agent scans recent customer interactions and transaction data to identify customers who have had a positive experience. These are the contacts most likely to leave a genuine, enthusiastic review — and least likely to feel put on the spot by a review request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Review Request">
    Once a happy customer is identified, the agent sends a review request through the most appropriate channel:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
        Sent to customers who interacted via web or email — a well-timed post-interaction email with a direct review link has high open and click-through rates.
      </Card>
      <Card title="SMS" icon="mobile">
        The highest-immediacy channel — a short, friendly text with a one-tap review link is ideal for service businesses where the customer interaction just concluded.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Chat" icon="comment">
        For customers still active in a chat session, the agent can extend a review request in context — capturing sentiment while the experience is fresh.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personalise the Message">
    Generic "please leave us a review" messages underperform. The Review Booster Agent personalises every outreach by including three key elements:

    - **Customer name** — addresses the customer directly, not as a bulk recipient
    - **Recent interaction** — references the specific service, purchase, or support experience they just had
    - **Appreciation** — expresses genuine thanks for their business before making any ask

    This personalisation signals that the request is meaningful, not automated spam — which drives significantly higher response rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Incoming Reviews">
    As reviews arrive, the agent tracks three monitoring metrics across your review channels:

    | Metric | What It Tells You |
    |---|---|
    | Reviews received | Total new reviews over any given period |
    | Ratings | Average star rating and distribution — identifies sentiment trends |
    | Customer feedback | The qualitative text of reviews — surfaces recurring themes, praise, and complaints |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to Reviews">
    The agent creates professional responses to incoming reviews — both positive and negative. Responses are crafted to acknowledge the customer's specific feedback, reinforce your brand voice, and demonstrate to prospective customers that you are engaged and responsive.

    Responding to every review is a known factor in Google's local ranking algorithm and in consumer trust-building — it signals an active, attentive business.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review Request Channels

<Accordion title="Email Requests">
  Email review requests work best when sent within 24–48 hours of a positive interaction. The agent personalises the subject line and body with the customer's name and a reference to their recent experience, and includes a direct link to your Google review page to eliminate friction.

  Email is the preferred channel for customers acquired online, through e-commerce, or through longer-cycle service engagements where a same-day SMS would feel premature.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="SMS Requests">
  SMS is the fastest channel for capturing review intent after an in-person or same-day service interaction. The message is kept brief — a sentence of appreciation, a sentence of context, and a short link. Response rates for SMS review requests are consistently higher than email because the message is seen immediately.

  SMS works best for restaurants, salons, contractors, clinics, and any business where the customer experience ends at a physical location or same-day service completion.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Chat Requests">
  When a customer is still active in a support or sales chat session and the conversation has gone well, the Review Booster Agent can extend an in-context review request. This is the most timely channel of all — the customer's positive experience is happening right now. A conversational, low-pressure ask at the end of a successful chat session captures sentiment that would otherwise disappear when the customer closes the window.
</Accordion>

## Personalised Message Elements

Every review request message is assembled from three components that make it feel personal and relevant:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Customer Name" icon="user">
    The customer's first name appears in the opening of the message. This single change — personalised vs. generic greeting — measurably increases open rates and response rates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent Interaction" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    A specific reference to what the customer just experienced. For example: "We hope your appointment on Tuesday went smoothly" or "Thanks for your recent order of [product]." This reference proves the message is not mass-broadcast spam.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Appreciation" icon="heart">
    A genuine expression of thanks for the customer's business, placed before any ask. Gratitude-first framing lowers psychological resistance and makes the review request feel like a natural extension of good service rather than a demand.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Monitoring Metrics

Once review requests are in flight, the agent tracks the following metrics to give you a clear picture of your reputation growth:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reviews Received" icon="star">
    The count of new reviews posted during any tracking period — your primary growth metric and the leading indicator of local search ranking improvement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ratings" icon="chart-bar">
    Average star rating and the distribution of 1–5 star reviews. Tracks whether sentiment is improving over time and flags individual negative reviews that may need a personalised response.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customer Feedback" icon="comments">
    The actual text of incoming reviews. The agent surfaces recurring themes — what customers praise most, what they flag as areas for improvement — turning review content into actionable intelligence.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Configuring Your Review Booster Agent

The Review Booster Agent is created from the **AI Agent Harbor** at `/members/agents`, the same as all other AI Surfer agents.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Agent Harbor">
    Go to `/members/agents` in your Members Dashboard and locate the **Create New AI Agent** form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the Agent">
    Set a clear **Agent name** (e.g. "Review Booster"), enter your **Business type** (e.g. "Local Service Business" or "Restaurant"), and write a **Purpose** statement that describes your review goals, your preferred channels, and any specific platforms (e.g. Google, Yelp) you want to prioritise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Launch AI Agent 🚀**. The agent is stored in Supabase with `status: "active"` and is ready to start building your online reputation immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Mention your primary review platform and the type of customer interactions you want to follow up on in the Purpose field. For example: "Focus on Google reviews, send requests after completed service appointments via SMS." The more specific the instruction, the more targeted the agent's outreach will be.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Review request messages should always reflect a genuine positive interaction. Sending automated review requests to customers who had a poor experience — before the issue is resolved — risks amplifying negative sentiment. Configure your agent's purpose statement to scope outreach to satisfied customers only.
</Warning>
