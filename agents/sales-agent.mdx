The AI Sales Agent is AI Surfer's front-line revenue worker. Powered by the `otd-sales-agent` skill, it acts as a virtual sales assistant for Ocean Tide Drop AI SURFER — engaging every website visitor in a natural conversation, uncovering their needs, and moving them toward a concrete next step. No lead form required, no human availability constraint, no gap in your sales coverage.

## Purpose

The Sales Agent exists to **convert website visitors into qualified leads and customers using AI conversations**. It runs at all hours, handles unlimited simultaneous conversations, and passes clean, qualified lead data to your CRM or Supabase pipeline the moment a visitor converts.

## Responsibilities

The agent is authorised to perform the following tasks on your behalf:

- Welcome website visitors with a friendly, personalised greeting
- Answer common questions about your products and services
- Identify each visitor's underlying business need
- Recommend the most relevant product or service match
- Capture lead information (name, email, phone, business needs)
- Schedule appointments directly in the conversation
- Follow up with prospects who did not convert on first contact

## Conversation Flow

Every sales conversation follows a five-stage flow designed to move visitors from curiosity to commitment without feeling scripted or pushy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Greeting">
    The agent opens with a friendly, personalised welcome message. The tone is warm and human — never robotic — and immediately signals that help is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discovery">
    The agent asks open-ended questions to understand what the visitor is actually looking for:

    - *What are you looking for?*
    - *What problem are you trying to solve?*
    - *What is your timeline?*

    These three questions surface intent, urgency, and context without feeling like an interrogation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualification">
    Once the visitor's needs are clear, the agent collects the information your sales team needs to follow up:

    | Field | Required |
    |---|---|
    | Name | Yes |
    | Email | Yes |
    | Phone | Optional |
    | Business needs | Yes |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Recommendation">
    Using the information gathered in Discovery and Qualification, the agent suggests the best-fit solution from your product or service catalogue. Recommendations are grounded in the visitor's stated needs — not a generic pitch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conversion">
    The agent presents a clear call to action matched to the visitor's readiness level:

    - **Appointment** — book a call with your team
    - **Free audit** — start with the AI Wave Audit
    - **Demo** — see the product in action
    - **Purchase** — proceed directly to checkout or onboarding
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Conversion Offers

The agent is equipped with four conversion offers it can extend depending on where the visitor is in their decision-making process:

<Accordion title="Appointment">
  The highest-intent offer — the agent invites the visitor to schedule a direct call or meeting with your team. Best used when the visitor has expressed a specific, urgent need and the qualification data is complete.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Free Audit">
  The AI Wave Audit is AI Surfer's no-cost entry point. Offering the audit works well for visitors who are interested but not yet ready to commit — it delivers immediate value and creates a natural follow-up touchpoint.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Demo">
  A product walkthrough offer for visitors who want to see capabilities before deciding. The agent positions the demo as low-commitment and high-value, removing the risk perception from moving forward.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Purchase">
  For visitors who arrive ready to buy, the agent can move them directly into your purchase or onboarding flow, reducing friction and capturing revenue before intent fades.
</Accordion>

## Configuring Your Sales Agent

Sales Agents are created and managed from the **AI Agent Harbor** at `/members/agents`. When you create a Sales Agent, the record is written to the Supabase `agents` table and becomes active immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to AI Agent Harbor">
    Open the Members Dashboard and go to `/members/agents`. You'll see the **Create New AI Agent** form at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the Agent Fields">
    Enter a descriptive **Agent name** (e.g. "Website Sales Bot"), set the **Business type** to match your industry (e.g. "Digital Marketing Agency"), and write a clear **Purpose** statement describing your sales goals and target audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the Agent">
    Click **Launch AI Agent 🚀**. The agent is inserted into Supabase with `status: "active"` and appears in your **Your AI Fleet** grid.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  All three fields — Agent name, Business type, and Purpose — are required before the agent can be launched. Leaving any field blank will prompt a validation alert.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Write a specific, detailed purpose statement. The more context you give the agent about your business, your ideal customer, and your conversion goals, the more relevant its conversations will be.
</Tip>
