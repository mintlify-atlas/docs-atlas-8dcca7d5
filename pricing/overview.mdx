AI Surfer is designed so you can start at zero cost and scale into fully managed AI infrastructure at whatever pace your business demands. The pricing structure has three distinct layers: free and low-cost tools for exploration and auditing, a subscription membership that unlocks the full platform, and one-time done-for-you build packages for businesses that want an expert team to implement their AI systems from the ground up.

## The Three Entry Points

Every AI Surfer journey begins with one of three on-ramps. Each is priced to match a specific stage of AI readiness — from curiosity to full commitment.

## Subscription Memberships

Beyond the three entry points, the platform offers five membership tiers — from the free **Starter Tide** (access level 0) up to the **Ocean Dominion** enterprise plan (access level 4) at $750/month. Each tier unlocks progressively more powerful AI agents, automation pipelines, and platform capabilities. See the full tier breakdown on the memberships page.

## Done-For-You AI Builds

For businesses that want AI systems **built for them**, not built by them, AI Surfer offers four implementation packages priced at $2,500 each as one-time engagements. These are not subscriptions — they are scoped build projects covering brand architecture, automation workflows, cinematic web design, or custom AI agent systems.
