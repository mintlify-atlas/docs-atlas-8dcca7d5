## How to Purchase

All four packages are available for direct purchase via Stripe Checkout. There are two ways to initiate a build:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the AI Surfer pricing page">
    Navigate to [otdaisurfer.surf](https://otdaisurfer.surf) and scroll to the **Done-For-You AI Builds** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your package">
    Each of the four packages has its own card with a full feature list. Choose the one that addresses your highest-priority gap — brand, automation, web, or agents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Build My System">
    Each card has a dedicated **"Build My System"** button that takes you directly to the Stripe Checkout page for that specific package. Payment is processed securely through Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your build brief">
    After purchase, you will be contacted by the AI Surfer team to begin scoping your engagement. The brief covers your business context, goals, and any existing assets that will be incorporated into the build.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are uncertain which package to start with, run the **free Wave Check** at `/wave-check` first. The diagnostic output often points directly at the category — brand, automation, web, or agents — that will deliver the most immediate impact for your business, making the choice straightforward.
</Tip>
