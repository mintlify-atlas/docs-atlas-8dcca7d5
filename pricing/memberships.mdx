AI Surfer's membership system gives every business a clear upgrade path from free access through enterprise-grade AI infrastructure. Each tier is assigned a numeric **access level** (0–4) that the platform uses to gate features — the higher your access level, the more of the AI agent suite, automation pipelines, and analytics dashboard become available to you. Subscriptions are managed through Stripe, and every paid tier has a dedicated `stripePriceId` used at checkout.

<Note>
  The live pricing on the AI Surfer website may differ from the standard PRICING constants during promotional periods. The current promoted offer is the **Builder Wave Members Portal** at **$17/month** — lower than the standard $29/month Builder Wave rate listed in the platform constants. Always check the [live pricing page](https://otdaisurfer.surf) for the most current offer before purchasing.
</Note>

## The Access Level System

Access levels are numeric integers (0–4) stored on the user's membership record and checked throughout the platform to determine which features, agents, and dashboards are unlocked. The access level gates are enforced server-side — a user cannot access a feature above their current level regardless of frontend state.

```
Level 0 → Starter Tide   — preview tools only
Level 1 → Builder Wave   — workflow templates + automation starters
Level 2 → Growth Wave    — full AI business system + analytics
Level 3 → Tsunami Pro    — multi-client layer + revenue tracking
Level 4 → Ocean Dominion — custom infrastructure + white-label + 1:1 support
```

<Info>
  Access levels are additive — each higher tier includes everything from the tiers below it. There is no feature available at Level 1 that is removed at Level 3.
</Info>

## Managing Your Subscription

All billing is handled through Stripe. After subscribing, you can manage your plan, update payment methods, and cancel or upgrade at any time through the Stripe customer portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to AI Surfer">
    Navigate to [otdaisurfer.surf](https://otdaisurfer.surf) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Access the billing or account settings page, which links directly to your Stripe customer portal session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your plan">
    From the Stripe portal you can upgrade tiers, update your payment method, download invoices, or cancel your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Downgrading your membership to a lower access level will immediately revoke access to features above that level. Export any data or automations you need to retain before downgrading.
</Warning>
